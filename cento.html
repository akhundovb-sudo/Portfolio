Cento

I designed and built Cento from the initial interface concepts to the final code. My goal was to make it easier to compare one amount across multiple currencies without switching pairs or repeating calculations.

Cento brings traditional currencies and crypto into one customizable list. Users choose their currencies, arrange them in their preferred order, and see conversions as they type.

I also designed widgets for quick access to exchange rates, charts, and crypto prices. A dedicated Travel widget uses location to identify the local currency and compare it with the user’s home currency.

Cento answers a simple everyday question: How much is this in my money?

Challenge

Comparing an amount across several currencies often means switching pairs and repeating calculations. I wanted to make that comparison effortless, whether checking crypto prices or understanding costs abroad.

Solution

I designed and built a single list for currencies and crypto. Enter an amount in any row and the others update instantly. Widgets bring rates and charts to the Home Screen, while a location-aware Travel widget compares the local currency with the user’s home currency.

Outcome

An iOS app that I took from interface design to working code, combining currency conversion, a built-in calculator, and widgets for everyday use and travel.

One list, any input

Any currency in the list can be the input. A user taps a row, enters an amount on the keypad, and the other rows convert as the amount is typed. Each row shows its rate, and there is no base currency to choose. To change the order, press and hold a row and drag it, no need to open Edit.

Currencies and crypto together

World currencies and cryptocurrencies live in the same list, so a user can compare dollars with bitcoin in one place. Rates are cached on the device, so the last known values are available offline.

Pull to refresh

Pull the list down to update the rates.

App icons

Settings has a picker with 16 app icons, some of them Pro. A tap on an icon changes it on the Home Screen, and the launch screen follows the chosen icon.

Widgets

Five widgets for the Home and Lock Screen: a currency pair, my currencies, a chart, the crypto market and a travel converter.

Pro

Free covers everyday use; Pro unlocks every currency, all crypto and extra icons. Subscription or one-time purchase, with a 7-day free trial.
