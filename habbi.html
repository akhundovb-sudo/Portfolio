Habbi

Habbi is an iOS habit-tracking app designed to help users build better routines, stay consistent, and track their progress over time.

I designed and developed the app from concept to App Store submission, focusing on a simple and intuitive experience that makes daily habit tracking effortless.

  • RoleProduct Designer & Vibe Coder
  • Period2026

Home

The screen users open every day, so it answers one question: what is left for today. A week strip sits at the top, with the habits for the chosen day below it. Each habit has a single control on the right: a tap checks it off, and habits with a goal like "3 times a day" fill a progress ring instead.

Create Habit

A new habit takes one screen and a few seconds. The keyboard is already open on the name field, and everything else has a sensible default: color, icon, repeat (every day), goal (1 time a day) and an optional reminder. Each setting is one row, so users can stop after the name or tune the rest.

Stats

Where users see whether the routine is holding. The current streak and the record sit at the top, followed by a month grid for every habit in its own color. Done days are filled and missed days are crossed out, so a gap is visible without reading any numbers.

Apple Health

Connected to Apple Health so users can automatically track habits based on steps, walking distance, active calories, and exercise minutes.
