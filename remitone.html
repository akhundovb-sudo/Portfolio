Remiton

Remiton is a mobile app for international money transfers. I designed the product from the ground up, turning a complex remittance flow into a simple mobile experience — from research and information architecture to prototyping, final UI and the design system.

  • RoleSole Product Designer
  • Scope0→1 Product Design
  • Period2022–2023

Challenge

Make international transfers simple while keeping rates, recipient details and payment status clear.

Solution

I designed a guided transfer flow with clear steps and a consistent interface.

Outcome

A mobile product designed from the ground up, with a clear three-step flow for sending money.

Login

I kept the returning-user experience intentionally lightweight: quick passcode access for everyday use, with clear recovery and support paths for edge cases.

Home

The home screen brings the primary money-transfer action and transaction history into one clear hierarchy. Users can start a transfer without navigating elsewhere, while recent activity provides immediate context and confidence.
