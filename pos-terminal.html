MyGuava POS Terminal

I designed a POS terminal interface for taking payments, viewing reports and managing transactions.

  • RoleLead Product Designer
  • Period2024–2025

Challenge

Keep everyday payments simple while making reports, refunds and settings easy to access.

Solution

I organized the interface into four sections: Sale, Reports, Transactions and More.

Outcome

One consistent interface for payments and daily terminal operations.

0Core sections0Payment methods

Sale Screen

Enter an amount using large buttons and a built-in calculator. Continue to payment with card, QR or cash, with card selected by default.

£0.00

Reports

View sales, tips, cancellations and refunds. Filter by date or payment method, then print or export a report.

Transactions

Find transactions using search and filters. View details, share receipts, issue full or partial refunds, or repeat a payment.

More

Access payment completion, pre-authorization, end-of-day reporting and terminal settings in a dedicated section.
